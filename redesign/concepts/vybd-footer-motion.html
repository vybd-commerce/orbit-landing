Footer motion.

Six ways to animate the Vybd footer. Option 01 is what's on the site now. The others start when they scroll into view; use Replay to watch again. Option 03 follows your cursor (or finger).

01Orbit (current)

The vybd.ai footer as it is now: a thin ring circles the wordmark with a mint planet, and a thicker arc slowly rocks behind it.

03Cursor spotlight

The wordmark is drawn as an outline. A spotlight follows the cursor and fills in the letters beneath it, echoing the cursor-reactive parcel in the hero. On phones it follows your finger, and drifts on its own when nobody is touching it.
