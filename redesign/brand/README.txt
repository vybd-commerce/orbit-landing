VYBD LOGO KIT  ·  Folded V

FILES
  vybd-logo.svg ................ Main logo (folded V + "Vybd"), for light backgrounds
  vybd-logo-on-dark.svg ........ Main logo for navy / dark backgrounds
  vybd-logo-mono-navy.svg ...... One-colour navy (print, stamps, embossing)
  vybd-logo-mono-white.svg ..... One-colour white (on photos, dark print)
  vybd-mark.svg ................ The folded V on its own (light backgrounds)
  vybd-mark-on-dark.svg ........ The folded V for dark backgrounds
  vybd-mark-mono-navy.svg / -white.svg ... One-colour V
  vybd-mark-animated.svg ....... The fold-in animation (loops every 6 s). Opens in any browser.
  vybd-wordmark.svg ............ "Vybd" on its own
  vybd-app-icon.svg ............ App icon / avatar (navy tile)
  png/ ......................... PNG exports at common sizes, transparent backgrounds,
                                 plus favicon-16/32/48 and a 180 px Apple touch icon

All text is converted to outlines, so the files don't need the font installed.

COLOURS
  Mint        #1BB59B   (light fold #5FD6BF, deep fold #13987F)
  Navy        #0f1b33   (light fold #25324d, on dark #3a4a6b)
  White       #FFFFFF

TYPE
  Wordmark    Instrument Serif (Google Fonts, free)
  Body        DM Sans

USE
  · Leave clear space around the logo of at least the width of one arm of the V.
  · Smallest size: logo 96 px wide on screen (25 mm in print); the V alone, 16 px.
  · Don't recolour the folds, stretch, rotate or add effects.
  · On photos or busy backgrounds, use the mono white version.
