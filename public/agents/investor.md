## What Vybd does

Vybd is one team for everything it takes to sell in the US. Brands from anywhere use it instead of hiring six separate companies for market research, regulations, freight, marketplaces, marketing and customer support. AI agents run routine work, specialists review complex cases, and the brand approves big decisions. All of it connects through one control tower.

## What it's building

- **Three layers.** One watches markets, rules and competitors; one coordinates stock, shipping, compliance and orders; one does the work. https://www.vybd.ai/product
- **Brand Brain** (in development, not for sale). One shared memory per brand that every agent reads from and writes back to, so each launch makes the next one smarter. https://www.vybd.ai/lab
- **Ecosystem.** A network of US market-entry partners (customs brokers, agencies, distributors) that brands' agents can find and hire, with agents for partners too. https://www.vybd.ai/ecosystem
