Today's topic

Let your AI introduce you

Pick any one prompt, paste it into the AI you use most, and post what it says about you. Read it before you post: if it reveals anything private, ask it to try again.

  1. 1. Introduce me to the room

    A witty friend introduces you: one roast, one compliment.

    Read the full prompt

    I'm at a meetup about how people use AI, and you're introducing me to the room. Based on what you know about me (memory, past chats, or just this chat), write a short introduction the way a witty friend would: who I am as an AI user, what I keep coming to you for, one gentle roast, and one genuine compliment. End with a question the person next to me can ask me.

    Keep it safe to share with strangers: no names, employers, health, money, relationships, or anything private. Under 100 words, written to be read out loud.

  2. 2. The duo

    You and your AI, introduced as a team.

    Read the full prompt

    I'm at a meetup about how people use AI, and you're introducing the two of us as a duo. Based on what you know about me (memory, past chats, or just this chat), tell the room who you are to me, who I am to you, and how we actually work together. Include one thing I do that drives you a little crazy and one thing you respect. End with a question the person next to me can ask us.

    Keep it safe to share with strangers: no names, employers, health, money, relationships, or anything private. Under 100 words, written to be read out loud.

  3. 3. The toast

    A 30-second best-friend toast about how you use AI.

    Read the full prompt

    I'm at a meetup about how people use AI, and you're giving a 30-second best-friend toast about me as an AI user. Based on what you know about me (memory, past chats, or just this chat), open with a story-like observation about how I use you, add one gentle roast, and finish on one sincere line. End with a question the person next to me can ask me.

    Keep it safe to share with strangers: no names, employers, health, money, relationships, or anything private. Under 100 words, written to be read out loud.

  4. 4. The speaker bio

    A mock-serious conference bio, and the talk you'd give.

    Read the full prompt

    I'm at a meetup about how people use AI, and you're writing my speaker bio using only what you've learned from how I use you, not my actual résumé. Based on what you know about me (memory, past chats, or just this chat), write it in the third person, in a mock-serious conference tone, with one gentle roast and one genuine compliment hidden in it. End with the title of the talk I'd obviously give.

    Keep it safe to share with strangers: no names, employers, health, money, relationships, or anything private. Under 100 words, written to be read out loud.

  5. 5. The nature documentary

    You, narrated as a species of AI user in the wild.

    Read the full prompt

    I'm at a meetup about how people use AI, and you're narrating me as a species of AI user being observed in the wild. Based on what you know about me (memory, past chats, or just this chat), describe my habitat, my feeding habits (what I keep coming to you for), and one strange behavior, in a hushed nature-documentary voice. Include one gentle roast and one genuine compliment. End with a question the person next to me can ask me.

    Keep it safe to share with strangers: no names, employers, health, money, relationships, or anything private. Under 100 words, written to be read out loud.
