## Direction contract

THESIS: The price of a product is a departures board. Every cost gets its own row, and "With Vybd" flips the rent rows to REMOVED in sequence. Refuses the SaaS split hero with a dashboard screenshot and a logo row.

OWN-WORLD: Board black (#111316) flap cells with off-white characters (#f2efe6), set on a cool concourse-gray ground (#e6e8eb) with black signage type. Color is rationed to the rows that change: signal yellow (#f6c445) marks rent, Vybd green (#1bb59b) marks the maker and Vybd. Archivo variable (condensed caps on flaps and headings, normal width for reading). Everything snaps to the flap-cell grid. Depth comes only from stacked flaps (a split line across each cell), with no drop shadows or gradients. "The usual chain" and "With Vybd" are two colorways of the same board.

STORY: The visitor sees where $40 goes today, watches rent disappear, then picks a path (Supply Chain or Vybd for the US), checks the promises, ledger, plan and pricing, and books or signs up.

Board flips row by row, top to bottom.
